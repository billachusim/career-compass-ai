# Career Compass AI

# Project Name

CareerBoost AI

# Vision

Build a modern, AI-powered career platform focused on helping job seekers optimize every stage of their job application process.

The platform should be SEO-first, mobile-first, extremely fast, and designed to rank on Google for thousands of career-related keywords.

The business model is Freemium with paid AI reports and premium subscriptions.

The UI should feel as polished as Linear, Notion, Stripe, and ChatGPT.
